H2: Finance report presentation template FAQ

Is the finance report presentation template editable?

Yes. All 12 slides are Smart Slides. Change your numbers, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the finance report presentation template?

Open the template and type your revenue, segment mix, operating expenses, cash position, and forecast into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the finance report presentation template for me?

Yes. Describe the finance report presentation in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the finance report presentation deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use a finance report presentation template?

Use the finance report presentation template for a recurring financial readout to executives, investors, or a board, such as a quarterly close, a budget review, or an annual performance summary.
