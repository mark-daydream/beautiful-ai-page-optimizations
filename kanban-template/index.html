H2: Overview

Many companies and organizations use a kanban board (also called a kanban system, or simply kanban) to visualize tasks and manage workflows. In a kanban board, each “card” represents a task, which can be organized and moved around based on their current progress. H2: Pro Tips for a Kanban Chart Template

Follow these tips when building out your own kanban chart template.

Label your columns appropriately A simple, traditional kanban uses three columns: to do, in progress, and done. However, you can add as many more columns as you need to represent your project stages.

Make sure your cards are clear Give each kanban card a concise, descriptive title. Anyone viewing the kanban at a glance should be able to easily understand the task.

Use limits to your advantage Put limits on the number of tasks in each column. Limits prevent overloaded workflows and encourage teams to prioritize and focus.

Assign your tasks promptly Assign each card to one or more team members as needed, and don’t forget to update your kanban flow as new tasks get created or completed.

H2: Kanban template FAQ

Is the kanban template editable?

Yes. The template is a fully editable Smart Slide. Change your columns, cards, and limits, and the layout adapts automatically as you work.

How do I add my own data to the kanban template?

Open the template and type your columns, cards, and limits in, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places them on the slide.

Can AI fill in the kanban template for me?

Yes. Describe the kanban you want to show and the AI assistant fills the template with your columns, cards, and limits in place. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the kanban template to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Slides export to PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link directly from Beautiful.ai.

When should I use a kanban board?

Use a kanban board when work moves through stages and the team needs to see what is in progress, what is waiting, and what is done, such as an engineering sprint, a support workflow, or any agile process.
