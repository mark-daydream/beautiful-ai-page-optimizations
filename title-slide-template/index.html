H2: Ideas for a Title Slide

Here are some design ideas to get you started:

Use your brand colors. The colors in your title slide should match your logo colors. People associate color with brands.

Bigger logos are not better. Your logo should not take precedence over the theme/title of the presentation. Size it to be visible but not the most prominent item on the title slide.

Balance the elements on the slide. Don’t overload the title slide with too much text or with too many graphics. Keep a balance between the elements so that there’s no competition for attention.

Why: Content kept word for word. The change is structural. The tab component becomes a stacked section so the copy no longer sits behind a click that crawlers can miss.

H2: Title slide template FAQ

Is the title slide template editable?

Yes. The template is a fully editable Smart Slide. Change your title, subtitle, and logo, and the layout adapts automatically as you work.

How do I add my own data to the title slide template?

Open the template and type your title, subtitle, and logo in, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places them on the slide.

Can AI fill in the title slide template for me?

Yes. Describe the title slide you want to show and the AI assistant fills the template with your title, subtitle, and logo in place. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the title slide template to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Slides export to PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link directly from Beautiful.ai.

When should I use a title slide?

Use a title slide to open a presentation, to mark a break between sections, and to close, so the deck has a clear start, clear chapters, and a clear end that carries your brand.
