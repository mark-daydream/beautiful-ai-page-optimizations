Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Beautiful.ai Claude Connector work?
MCP, or Model Context Protocol, is a standard that lets AI assistants connect with external applications using approved tools. The Beautiful.ai Claude Connector uses it to give Claude scoped access to your Beautiful.ai account, so it can search decks, create presentations from structured outlines, generate from templates, and export finished files, all on your behalf.

Can Claude make presentations?
Yes. With the Beautiful.ai Connector enabled, Claude can turn a prompt, outline, or pasted document into a full Beautiful.ai presentation, with each slide typed and structured rather than dumped as a wall of text. The deck is built on Smart Slide layouts, so it arrives designed, not just drafted.

What can I create with the connector?
Anything you'd build in Beautiful.ai: pitch decks, board updates, QBRs, recap decks, product launches. Prompt from scratch, generate from one of your templates, or ask for a specific look. Generation options include image sources (AI, web, stock, or none) and a language setting for non-English decks.

Can Claude edit my existing Beautiful.ai decks?
The connector can find, open, summarize, copy from templates, and export your existing presentations, but it doesn't edit them slide by slide. For hands-on changes, open the deck in Beautiful.ai. That's by design: drafting happens in Claude, editing and polish happen in the editor built for it.

Is the connector secure?
Authentication runs on OAuth 2.0, and neither Claude nor the connector receives or stores your Beautiful.ai password. The connector follows your account's existing permission model, so private decks stay private. Beautiful.ai's security program is SOC 2 Type II certified and aligned with GDPR, CCPA, and PCI.

Do I need a paid Beautiful.ai account?
You need a Beautiful.ai account that can complete the OAuth sign-in. New to Beautiful.ai? Pro and Team both offer a 14-day free trial, so you can test the whole Claude-to-deck workflow before paying.

How is this different from the Beautiful.ai ChatGPT integration?
Beautiful.ai also integrates with ChatGPT, alongside Slack, PowerPoint, and Webex. The Claude Connector is built on MCP and runs inside Claude's Connectors Directory, while the ChatGPT integration lives in ChatGPT. Same idea in both places: prompt in chat, get designed slides, finish in Beautiful.ai.
