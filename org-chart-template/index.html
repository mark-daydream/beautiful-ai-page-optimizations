H2: What is an org chart, and when should you use one?

An org chart, or an organizational chart, is a diagram that illustrates the structure and reporting relationships within a company. Org charts are great tools for displaying how work flows within the company. With our org chart template slides, you can add different slides to any presentation to showcase how departments are connected, who reports to whom, and share contact information. Org chart slides are also great for management, who need insight into hours per department, promotions, and team resources.

You might use our org chart slide templates to:

• Help people understand company structure  • Keep track of changing roles or employees  • Share employee contact information company-wide

H2: Tips for Adding an Org Chart Slide

Keep these do’s and don’ts in mind when putting together an org chart slide.

Do choose the right type of org chart. Structural charts are simple and minimalistic, functional charts display departmental responsibilities, and position charts list employee titles and duties. Pick a style that works for your organization’s size and needs.

Don’t forget to update your org chart regularly. Employees will check your org chart when they need important information. Check on it regularly so they’re not using outdated information.

Do use different fonts, colors, and sizes. Make your org chart easy to read by adjusting the fonts, colors, and sizes.

Don’t be afraid to create several charts. One giant org chart can be overwhelming to look at. Create smaller org chart slides broken down by division or department if needed.

H2: Org chart template FAQ

Is the org chart template editable?

Yes. Open it with Use this template and every element is editable in the Beautiful.ai editor: the people, their titles, the reporting lines, and the colors and fonts around them. Smart Slides keep the hierarchy aligned as you make changes.

How do I add my own team?

Click Use this template, then type names, roles, and reporting lines directly into the chart. You can also describe your team and let the AI assistant build the structure for you.

Can AI fill it in for me?

Yes. Describe the team and the AI assistant can draft supporting text, summarize each role from its title and department, and generate a short overview to sit alongside the chart.

Can I export to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Share a live link, present directly from Beautiful.ai, or export the finished slide to PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF.

When should I use an org chart?

Use an org chart when people need to see structure at a glance: how departments connect, who reports to whom, and where to find a colleague’s contact information. It also gives managers a working view of roles as they change.
