H2: Tips to create an effective brand guidelines presentation

When you use this template to craft your brand guidelines presentation, keep these four tips in mind:

Create a theme With a brand guidelines presentation template, you’re likely using all of your branded colors, fonts and logos to create the deck. Making a custom theme will make it easier to recreate branded decks moving forward without having to manually adjust fonts and colors on each slide.

Make It Engaging This presentation is all about your brand, so it should be engaging. Don’t be afraid to include examples of images and icons that align with your brand’s story.

Keep it Concise This doesn’t need to be a 20 slide deck explaining why you landed on specific typography. Include only the most relevant pieces of content to help colleagues stay on-brand and consistent in their roles.

Add Personality If any deck should have personality, it’s a brand guidelines presentation. Think outside of the box and try different layouts to present your branding to stakeholders.

H2: Brand guidelines template FAQ

Is the brand guidelines template editable?

Yes. All 8 slides are Smart Slides. Change your brand, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the brand guidelines template?

Open the template and type your logo details, colors, typography, photography, and social assets into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the brand guidelines template for me?

Yes. Describe the brand guidelines in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the brand guidelines deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use a brand guidelines presentation?

Use a brand guidelines presentation when teams need one reference for logo use, color palettes, typography, and photography, at onboarding, before a campaign, or whenever brand assets are shared with a partner.
