H2: Tips to design an impactful mood board presentation

As you use this template to craft your mood board presentation, keep these do’s and don’ts in mind:

Use a Photo Grid Most mood boards will include a visual collage of images, text, and samples. Our photo grid slide template is a great option for showcasing a variety of inspirational photos related to your project.

Make It Engaging Mood boards are meant to inspire, so make sure your presentation is engaging from beginning to end. Include plenty of photos and videos, and don’t be afraid to play around with animations to bring your board to life.

Don’t forget to provide context Without context, your mood board could look like a hodgepodge of random images. Provide your audience with background information on the project and your thought process and vision behind the mood board.

Add Personality Mood boards shouldn’t lack personality. You may want to opt for bold colors and unique fonts to make your mood board really pop.

H2: Mood board template FAQ

Is the mood board template editable?

Yes. All 10 slides are Smart Slides. Change your inspiration, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the mood board template?

Open the template and type your background, color theme, hex codes, photography, and style samples into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the mood board template for me?

Yes. Describe the mood board in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the mood board deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use a mood board presentation?

Use a mood board presentation at the start of a project or a new venture, when you gather inspiration for a design direction and need the team or external partners to react to colors, photography, and style in one place.
