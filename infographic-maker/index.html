New FAQ (8 questions)
New

Frequently asked questions

How do I make an infographic with Beautiful.ai?
Add the Infographic Slide to a deck, type your data points in, and let the layout realign as you go. Apply your Theme so it matches the rest of the presentation, then present it or export it. The three steps above walk through it in full.

What is an infographic?
An infographic presents information visually so a reader can take in the shape of it before reading a word: a sequence of steps, a comparison, a hierarchy, or a set of figures with the relationships between them made explicit. In a presentation, its job is to let an audience follow your point at a glance while you talk.

What types of infographics can I make?
Process and step sequences with the Infographic Slide or Sequence, cycles with The Arrow Cycle or Cycle, hierarchies with Pyramid or Hub and Spoke, funnels with Horizontal Funnel, and relationship maps with Connection Diagrams. For number-led graphics, the Data and Chart slide templates cover bar charts, line charts, and dashboards.

Is the infographic maker free?
There is no free plan. Beautiful.ai runs a 14-day free trial of the Pro or Team plan, which includes the Infographic Slide and every other Smart Slide layout, and Pro is $14.50 per month after the trial. If you need a free tool for a one-off graphic, several of the standalone infographic makers offer one.

Can I use my infographic in PowerPoint or Google Slides?
Yes. Export any presentation as a PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF file, with your layouts and design integrity intact. Slides remain fully editable after export, so the infographic arrives as a working slide rather than a pasted picture. Google Slides downloads are available too.

Can Beautiful.ai build an infographic from my data?
AI can summarize the data on an infographic slide, find trends and relationships inside it, refine the copy, and create a supporting image. You can also start a whole deck from a prompt, a pasted outline, or your own source material, and review the slide-by-slide outline before any slides are generated.

[NEEDS CUSTOMER INPUT] Can I download my infographic as an image for social media? Customer-owned sources document PowerPoint, PDF, and Google Slides export only. No image-file export is documented anywhere on the site, and all three named competitors offer one. Confirm whether this exists before this question is answered. Excluded from the FAQPage schema until then.

When is a dedicated infographic tool a better fit?
When the infographic is the finished artifact rather than part of a presentation. A standalone graphic for a blog post, a social feed, or a print handout is what tools like Canva, Venngage, and Piktochart are built around, and each of them downloads the finished result as an image file for exactly that. Beautiful.ai is the better fit when the graphic has to live in a deck, stay on brand with the slides around it, and still be editable when the numbers change.
