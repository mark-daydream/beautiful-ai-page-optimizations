New FAQ section (+ FAQPage schema)
New

HR presentation template FAQ

Which HR presentation template should you use?

Start with who is in the room. For a new hire, use the 30-60-90, employee handbook, or new hire onboarding template. For current employees, the company culture, training, and HR benefits templates carry the sessions HR runs every year. For reviews between a manager and an employee, use the performance review or year end employee review template. The recruitment presentation is for candidates, and the company overview works for annual meetings, investor pitches, and new hire orientations.

What should an HR presentation include?

It depends on the meeting, but the decks on this list share a few slides: the company’s history, values, and mission, the policies or expectations that apply to the audience, the benefits and perks on offer, and who to contact with questions. Photos of the team, charts, and timelines help the information stick.

What are good HR presentation topics?

The topics HR teams present most often are the ones above: onboarding and 30-60-90 plans, company culture and values, training, performance and year end reviews, the employee handbook and company policies, benefits and open enrollment, recruiting, and the company overview. Compliance training and compensation are the other two subjects HR is asked to explain, and both fit the training and HR benefits templates.

How do you make an HR presentation for employees?

Start with what employees need to know first, then work down to secondary details. Keep each slide to the most relevant points, and use bullet lists, charts, and images to make text-heavy subjects like benefits or policies easier to digest. Include links to provider websites and contact information for HR, and leave pauses for questions.

Can you customize these HR presentation templates?

Yes. Every template on this list is editable in Beautiful.ai. Smart Slides keep everything aligned and sized as you add your own content, a brand theme applies your company’s colors, fonts, and logo, and the finished deck exports to PowerPoint.

HR presentation template FAQ +FAQ schema

Which HR presentation template should you use?

Start with who is in the room. For a new hire, use the 30-60-90, employee handbook, or new hire onboarding template. For current employees, the company culture, training, and HR benefits templates carry the sessions HR runs every year. For reviews between a manager and an employee, use the performance review or year end employee review template. The recruitment presentation is for candidates, and the company overview works for annual meetings, investor pitches, and new hire orientations.

What should an HR presentation include?

It depends on the meeting, but the decks on this list share a few slides: the company’s history, values, and mission, the policies or expectations that apply to the audience, the benefits and perks on offer, and who to contact with questions. Photos of the team, charts, and timelines help the information stick.

What are good HR presentation topics?

The topics HR teams present most often are the ones above: onboarding and 30-60-90 plans, company culture and values, training, performance and year end reviews, the employee handbook and company policies, benefits and open enrollment, recruiting, and the company overview. Compliance training and compensation are the other two subjects HR is asked to explain, and both fit the training and HR benefits templates.

How do you make an HR presentation for employees?

Start with what employees need to know first, then work down to secondary details. Keep each slide to the most relevant points, and use bullet lists, charts, and images to make text-heavy subjects like benefits or policies easier to digest. Include links to provider websites and contact information for HR, and leave pauses for questions.

Can you customize these HR presentation templates?

Yes. Every template on this list is editable in Beautiful.ai. Smart Slides keep everything aligned and sized as you add your own content, a brand theme applies your company’s colors, fonts, and logo, and the finished deck exports to PowerPoint.
