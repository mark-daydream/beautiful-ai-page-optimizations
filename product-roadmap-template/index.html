H2: Product roadmap template FAQ

Is the product roadmap template editable?

Yes. All 10 slides are Smart Slides. Change your roadmap, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the product roadmap template?

Open the template and type your vision, strategy, goals, product plan, timelines, and key results into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the product roadmap template for me?

Yes. Describe the product roadmap in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the product roadmap deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use a product roadmap presentation?

Use a product roadmap presentation when you pitch what is coming to investors or customers, brief the sales team on upcoming products, or show a development team the trajectory of the work.
