H2: Helpful hints for creating effective case studies that convert

A thoughtful presentation can set your brand above the rest. Consider these tips when organizing your case study.

Include engaging images Add visual interest to your presentation by including charts, graphs, and photos. This will help break up your data and make your presentation memorable.

Use white space Content heavy presentations work well with simple clean slides that don’t overwhelm your audience. Feel confident leaving blank space on each slide.

Tell a story with data Teammates and clients alike will stay engaged if you use your data to drive a story. Use compelling information to demonstrate your value with numbers and testimonials that manifest client success.

Feature actionable information The data you showcase should drive action. What outcome are you aiming for by giving this presentation? Who is your audience? Keep your viewers in mind and make sure your presentation is leading them towards your desired outcome.

Why: Content kept word for word. The change is structural. The tab component becomes a stacked section so the copy no longer sits behind a click that crawlers can miss.

H2: Case study template FAQ

Is the case study template editable?

Yes. All 15 slides are Smart Slides. Change your customer's story, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the case study template?

Open the template and type the challenge, the solution, and the results into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the case study template for me?

Yes. Describe the case study in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the case study deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use a case study presentation?

Use a case study presentation when a prospect is close to a decision and wants proof, or when you want to up-sell a current client with a peer's results. It also works as a compilation of several customer stories in one deck.

Why: Answers are product facts in this page’s own nouns; the “when should I use” answer derives from the kept overview copy, so it stays unique to the page. No answer claims the template or the product is free. Mirrored in the FAQPage schema below.
