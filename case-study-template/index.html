H2: What is a case study, and when should you use one?

A case study addresses a customer’s challenge and shows the solution your product or service delivered, with the results illustrated by real data. It is a form of social proof: when prospects see how an existing customer uses your product to solve the same problem they have, your brand earns their confidence and the lead becomes easier to convert. A case study slide holds that story in one place: the customer, the challenge, the solution, and the outcome with a number or two behind it.

A case study template earns its place wherever social proof does the persuading: sales enablement decks sent to prospective clients, the decision-making stage of a buyer’s journey, an upsell conversation with a current customer, or a success story your team wants on the record. Build a standalone case study slide for each customer, or compile several into one presentation. Whether you need a single case study slide inside a larger deck or a template for a case study that stands on its own, this is an editable case study template for presentations: the design is done, so you only add your customer’s story.

Why: The question-form H2 follows the spec, and the education is Beautiful.ai’s own: the definition, the social-proof framing, the sales-enablement use, and the standalone-or-compiled choice all come from the company’s case study writing. The closing sentence carries the supporting terms (case study slide, template for a case study) in natural phrasing. Sources: C1, C2, C4, C5, C11, C12, C13, C14

H2: Pro tips for case study slides

Keep these tips in mind when customizing your case study slide:

Tell a story with data Teammates and clients alike stay engaged when your data drives a story. Use the numbers and testimonials that show the customer’s success to demonstrate your value.

Include engaging images Charts, graphs, and photos add visual interest, break up your data, and make the slide memorable.

Use white space Content-heavy presentations work well with simple, clean slides that don’t overwhelm your audience. Feel confident leaving blank space on the slide.

Feature actionable information The data you showcase should drive action. Decide what outcome you want from the presentation and who the audience is, and keep the slide leading them toward that outcome.

Why: All four tips come from Beautiful.ai’s own case study guidance, laid out as a stacked section per the spec so crawlers reach them without a tab click. Sources: C7, C8, C9, C10

New Case study template FAQ (5 questions)
New

H2: Case study template FAQ

Is the case study template editable?

Yes. Every element is editable in the Beautiful.ai editor. Swap the customer logo and quote, change the results number and the chart behind it, and adjust colors and labels to your theme, and Smart Slides keep the layout aligned as you work.

How do I add my own customer story?

Type the customer’s challenge, your solution, and the outcome straight onto the slide, or hand the work to AI: describe the customer in a prompt, or upload a document or data file and the AI will use it to fill in the case study. Smart Slides handle the alignment as the content lands.

Can AI fill it in for me?

Yes. Describe the customer and the result in a prompt and the AI assistant fills the template automatically. It can also rewrite, expand, or shorten the text on the slide and generate speaker notes from your content. Paid plans include unlimited AI content generation.

Can I export to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Export the finished case study as an editable PowerPoint (PPT) file, a PDF, or Google Slides, so the same slide works as a case study PowerPoint template for teams that present there. Transitions and animations stay behind in Beautiful.ai, so present from your dashboard or share a live link when you want the full animated version.

When should I use a case study?

Use a case study when social proof does the persuading: a sales deck sent to a prospect, a buyer at the decision-making stage, an upsell conversation with a current customer, or a success story you want on the record. When you need the full arc with company background, audience, product overview, and several data slides, the case study presentation template covers it as a complete deck.

Why: The spec’s five fixed questions with this page’s nouns. The editability answer carries the documented Smart Slide behavior, the export answer names PPT for the searchers who phrase it that way and states the animation boundary honestly, and the when-should-you answer derives from the overview copy and hands the full-deck intent to the existing presentation template.
