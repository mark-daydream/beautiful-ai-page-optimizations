H2: What is an infographic slide, and when should you use one?

Need to dress up a plain list of data? An infographic presentation slide displays your data in an interesting, impactful way. Instead of adding a regular bullet list to a slide, an infographic slide uses arrow bars to organize your data. Arrows can imply relationships or movement between data points, explain steps to achieve a goal, or make any list in your presentation look well-designed.

Use our infographic slide template to:

• Share important data visually  • Lay out steps in a process  • Show relationships between different data

Why: The existing overview copy is real, useful content and stays in full; only the heading changes to the question searchers ask, and the block moves below the new template layer.

H2: Pro Tips for an Infographic Presentation Slide

Use an infographic presentation template to visually impart information. Make the most of this slide by following these suggested steps.

Label your infographic slide appropriately If you’re explaining steps in a process in your infographic slide, number each arrow. Comparing percentages? Place the percentage in or around each arrow.

Clearly represent your data A longer arrow bar implies a larger number or percentage. Make sure the size of each bar in your list is proportional to your data.

Get creative Give each arrow a unique color, add animations or transitions, or play around with fonts and sizing to make your infographic slide template pop.

Use it for more than one data point or idea One arrow in your infographic slide will look unfinished or like a mistake. Because it’s meant to stylize a list, make sure you have multiple points or steps to display.

H2: Infographic template FAQ

Is the infographic template editable?

Yes. Open it with Use this template and every element is editable in the Beautiful.ai editor: the arrow bars, their labels, and the colors and fonts around them. Smart Slides keep the bars aligned as you make changes.

How do I add my own content?

Click Use this template, then type your steps or data points into the arrow bars. You can also describe the list and let the AI assistant fill it in.

Can AI fill it in for me?

Yes. The AI assistant can fill the arrow bars from a description, summarize the underlying data, and call out trends worth annotating.

Can I export to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Share a live link, present directly from Beautiful.ai, or export the finished slide to PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF.

When should I use an infographic slide?

Use an infographic slide when a plain list undersells the content: steps toward a goal, movement between data points, or any list that deserves better than bullets.

Why: Answers are product facts in this page’s own nouns; the “when should I use” answer derives from the kept overview copy, so it stays unique to the page. No answer claims the template or the product is free; pricing intent is answered by the trial. Mirrored in the FAQPage schema below.
