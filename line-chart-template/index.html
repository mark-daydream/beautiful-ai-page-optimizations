H2: Overview

A line chart is a concise, visual way to show trends over time. The vertical yaxis on a line chart (also called a line graph) is usually the amount of something, such as dollars, number of sales, or number of customers. The horizontal x-axis typically uses time as a measurement. It could be years, months, weeks, days, or even hours.

A line chart example can be used to plot and display any kind of relationship between two values. How many conversions a marketing campaign received over a year, how many sales occurred in the last quarter, and how social media engagement performed in the last few weeks are all examples.

H2: Pro Tips for Using Line Chart Templates

A few things to keep in mind when designing a line chart template:

Create a key or legend. If you’re comparing multiple items and using several lines on your chart, be sure to identify what each line represents by color.

Don’t forget to label each axis. Label each axis with the numeric value or unit of measurement that you’ve used.

Use the best unit of measurement. Make sure the unit of measurement you choose will show obvious changes in your lines. For example, using time in months may show changes more clearly than time in weeks.

Don’t forget to give your line chart a title. What information is your line chart displaying? Give it a title so your audience knows what they’re looking at instantly.

H2: Line chart template FAQ

Is the line chart template editable?

Yes. The template is a fully editable Smart Slide. Change your values, labels, and time periods, and the layout adapts automatically as you work.

How do I add my own data to the line chart template?

Open the template and type your values, labels, and time periods in, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places them on the slide.

Can AI fill in the line chart template for me?

Yes. Describe the line chart you want to show and the AI assistant fills the template with your values, labels, and time periods in place. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the line chart template to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Slides export to PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link directly from Beautiful.ai.

When should I use a line chart?

Use a line chart when you need to show how a value moves over time, such as conversions across a year, sales by quarter, or engagement week by week. For a single point in time or parts of a whole, a bar graph or pie chart reads more clearly.
