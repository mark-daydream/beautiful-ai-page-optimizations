Existing Template Pages: the optimization spec for every "[x] template" page

One CMS template drives all 236 template pages, so this sheet is written the way that system works: section by section, what is locked on every page, and what fills in per page. It optimizes the existing pages (no net-new landing pages), and every video stays intact. Two pages are already instantiated on this contract (/templates/pie-chart and /templates/swot-analysis), and Batch 1 applies it to 10 chart pages carrying 82,330 monthly searches on their "[x] template" head terms.

Derived from live scrapes of the template family (re-verified 2026-07-24/25), Search Console evidence across all ten Batch 1 pages, and the winning-SERP pattern (Canva, Adobe Express, Venngage). Each section shows the layout, the fill rule, and both real instantiations. Before/after annotated mockups: pie chart · SWOT analysis.

Fixed Four steps, every page

  • 1. Open the template. "Use this template" opens it in the Beautiful.ai editor.
  • 2. Add your [data/content]. Type it in, upload a document or data file, or describe it and let AI fill the template.
  • 3. Make it yours. Adjust colors and labels; Smart Slides keep the layout aligned as you edit.
  • 4. Present or export. Live link, present in Beautiful.ai, or export to PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF.
  • Every step is a documented product behavior; no invented click-paths.

Variable Per page

  • Step 2's nouns: what "your data" means on this template, in its own vocabulary.

FAQ + programmatic links

New Job: answer the long-tail questions Search Console already records ("editable pie chart template"), power FAQPage schema, and make the 236-page library crawl like a connected system.
H2 · [x] template FAQ

Fixed Five questions, every page

  • Is the [x] template editable? · How do I add my own [data/content]? · Can AI fill it in for me? · Can I export to PowerPoint or Google Slides? · When should I use a [x]?
  • Answers are 2–3 sentences of product facts only; the "when should you use" answer derives from the page's kept overview copy, so it is unique by construction.
  • Pricing questions answer honestly with the 14-day trial framing; no answer calls the template or the product free.
  • Below the FAQ, a rule-generated "More [category] templates" text module: every same-category sibling with its "[x] template" head term as anchor text, plus the category hub and one product page. Visible breadcrumbs on every page.

Variable Per page

  • The nouns in every question and answer, and the sibling-link set (category-driven).

Both instantiations

/templates/pie-chart: 5 Qs · links to 10 data & chart siblings + hub
/templates/swot-analysis: 5 Qs · comparison-category siblings + hub

Schema package (generated per page from the same slots)

  • Keep: Organization (site-wide).
  • Add: BreadcrumbList · HowTo (mirrors the four steps) · FAQPage (mirrors the five answers verbatim) · VideoObject for the kept hero and walkthrough videos, with name, description, thumbnail, and upload date per video, mirroring the video-indexing work the Beautiful.ai team is adding in code.
  • Revise: the existing WebPage/CreativeWork block's url host (beautiful.ai vs the www canonical); one Webflow template edit, shipped with the title-suffix fix.

Two CMS realities this spec is built around

  • Sections propagate. These pages are CMS templates: a section added to one page appears on all of them. So no section ships until its content exists for every affected page. The weekly batches fill content page by page, but structural changes wait for full coverage (or for a parallel-template setup, which the Beautiful.ai dev team is checking on).
  • Videos stay intact on every page. The hero and carousel videos demo the templates' built-in animations and are kept everywhere. The optimization layer adds around them: poster thumbnails with metadata, VideoObject schema, descriptive anchors, lazy loading.

Rules that hold on every batch

  • Grammar gate: every filled slot reads as natural English; grammar wins over exact-match phrasing, including on kept headings.
  • Unique-content floor: the 10 fills in a batch are diffed against each other; any sentence repeated across pages must be a fixed product fact.
  • Intent gate: supporting terms need ≥30% SERP overlap with the "[x] template" head term. "Maker" and "generator" terms are excluded whatever their volume, because their SERPs are won by in-page tools.
  • Honesty rule: competitors lead with "free"; Beautiful.ai is trial-based and the copy never claims free.

Weekly batch cycle

  • Monday: scrape each live page, fill the spec for the week's 10 pages, run the grammar gate and unique-content diff, publish to the review hub.
  • By Wednesday: Beautiful.ai reviews; rejections carry a reason and are revised in-cycle.
  • Thursday/Friday: approved copy into Webflow (direct entry with editor access, or a paste-ready sheet per page).
  • Batch 1 (10 chart pages, 82,330 searches/mo): pie chart, flow chart, timeline, word cloud, Venn diagram, bar graph, infographic, Gantt chart, SWOT analysis, org chart. Batches 2–12 re-ranked by head-term volume. Position + click checks at 4 and 8 weeks against the captured Search Console baseline.
