Frequently asked questions

How do I make an org chart in Beautiful.ai?

Add the Org Chart slide to a presentation, click any person, and use the plus buttons on their four sides to add peers, managers, and direct reports. Then set the node style, direction, and layout from the properties panel. You can also start from the org chart template and edit from there.

Is the org chart maker free?

Beautiful.ai runs on a 14-day free trial of Pro or Team, which covers the org chart slide and every other Smart Slide. Paid plans start at $14.50 a month for Pro, billed annually. There is no free tier. See pricing for the full comparison.

Can I use my org chart in PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans export the deck as an editable PowerPoint file, a Google Slides file, or a PDF. Beautiful.ai’s transitions and animations are not supported by those platforms, so they are lost on export. A shareable link keeps the presentation intact.

What types of org charts can I build?

Structural charts stay simple and minimalistic, functional charts display departmental responsibilities, and position charts list employee titles and duties. Pick the one that suits your organization’s size and needs, then use the node style and layout properties to match.

Can I put team photos on the chart?

Three of the five node styles carry photos. Photo 1 is the Box style with a photo, Photo 2 is the Minimal style with a photo, and Photo 3 is a circle style with a photo. Minimal and Box cover the cases where you want a compact, high-density chart instead.

What is an org chart?

An org chart, or organizational chart, is a diagram that illustrates the structure and reporting relationships within a company. Teams use them to show how departments connect, who reports to whom, and how to reach people. They also give management a view of roles, promotions, and team resources.

How large an org chart fits on one slide?

The Layout property handles most of it. Expanded and Collapsed suit charts of different sizes, Stacked puts nodes directly on top of each other, and Table uses the full slide when you do not need photos. Past a certain size, split it. Smaller charts broken down by division or department read better than one giant chart.

When is a dedicated org chart tool a better fit?

When the chart is your system of record. Lucidchart sells “Automatically up-to-date org charts” and Creately’s org chart software leads on turning “Spreadsheet Data Into a Living Org Chart Fast”, both built to stay in sync with people data between meetings. Beautiful.ai is built for the meeting itself, where the chart has to sit in the deck, match the brand, and hold up on a screen.

Can I build the chart from a spreadsheet or our HR system?
