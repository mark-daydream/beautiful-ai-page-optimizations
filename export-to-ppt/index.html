Optimized H2 — Exporting an editable PowerPoint file (steps cleanup)
Before

Exporting an editable PowerPoint file

[Embedded video — "Export to PowerPoint from Beautiful.ai," Vimeo]

If your Beautiful.ai deck is done, but you need to present in PowerPoint, here's what you need to do:

  1. To export the entire deck at once open up the presentation you wish to export.
  2. Next, click Actions at the top left panel.
  3. Select the Export Presentation tab and choose Editable PowerPoint.
  4. Click Export to save a copy of the editable PPT file to your device.

An exporting progress box should appear. Once the export is complete, it should download automatically.

To export a single slide as an editable PowerPoint, follow these three steps:

  1. click the More option that appears on the left or right.
  2. A pop-up window will launch and you can select the Export Slide button.
  3. Then, choose Export Slide to PPT.

Once the export is complete, the slide— or slides— it should download automatically.

Your PPT file is fully editable, so you can make any last minute changes or edits in the PowerPoint platform.

What transfers to PowerPoint (and what doesn't)

The export produces a fully editable .pptx file, not a flattened set of images. Your slides, text, and layouts come through ready to edit in PowerPoint.

New H2 — Exporting from Beautiful.ai FAQs (4 Q&As)
New

Exporting from Beautiful.ai FAQs

Does Beautiful.ai export to PowerPoint?

Yes. Beautiful.ai exports presentations as editable PowerPoint (.pptx) files. You can export an entire deck from the Actions menu or a single slide from its More menu, and the file downloads automatically once the export completes.

Is the exported PowerPoint file editable?

Yes. The export is a fully editable PPTX file, so you can make final changes directly in PowerPoint. Keep in mind that audio files and animations from your Beautiful.ai deck won't transfer over.

What export formats does Beautiful.ai support?

Beautiful.ai supports editable PowerPoint export and PDF export. You can also skip exporting altogether and present directly from Beautiful.ai, including offline playback.

Which plans include PowerPoint export?

PowerPoint import and export are included in the Pro plan and above. If you want to test the workflow first, the 14-day free trial of Pro or Team gives you full access to the plan's features, including editable PowerPoint export.
