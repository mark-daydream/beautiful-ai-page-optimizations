Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a PDF to Google Slides with Beautiful.ai?
Start with Create with AI, attach the PDF using the plus icon, and review the outline it drafts from your content. Generate the deck, then choose Google Slides under Export Presentation. The finished deck opens in Google Slides as soon as the export completes.

Is the PDF to Google Slides converter free?
There is no free plan. Beautiful.ai runs a 14-day free trial of the Pro or Team plan, and exporting to Google Slides is included on Pro, Team, and Enterprise. After the trial, Pro is $14.50 per month billed annually.

Can I edit the presentation in Google Slides after export?
Yes. Exports to Google Slides are fully editable. The deck arrives in Google Slides as working slides, so you can keep changing text, images, and layouts there.

Will my PDF’s formatting be preserved?
No, and that is by design. Beautiful.ai does not trace the PDF page for page. It rebuilds the content into designed slides, and the outline step lets you decide what carries over. If you need an exact visual copy, Adobe’s own guidance is to convert the PDF to PowerPoint first and import that file into Google Slides, and it notes the formatting may change along the way. If you need a deck you can edit, restyle, and present, start here.

What does not carry over to Google Slides?
Google Slides does not support Beautiful.ai’s embedded videos, audio files, animations, or transitions, so those are lost on export. Everything else exports as editable slides. To keep the animations, present from Beautiful.ai or share a link to the live presentation.

What if I see “This app is blocked” when I export?
That message means your company’s Google Workspace is blocking the connection. Ask your Google Workspace admin to open API Controls under Security, choose Manage Third-Party App Access, find Beautiful.ai, and change its access to Trusted. Once Beautiful.ai is a trusted app, the export runs.

Can I export the same deck to PowerPoint or PDF as well?
Yes. The same Export Presentation menu offers editable PowerPoint, PowerPoint images, PDF, print, and an Offline Player package. Editable PowerPoint keeps every element editable, speaker notes included. If PowerPoint is your destination, the PDF to PPT page walks through that flow.
