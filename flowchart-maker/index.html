Frequently Asked Questions

What is a flowchart?
A flowchart is a diagram that shows the sequence of steps and decisions needed to perform a process. Each step sits inside a diagram shape, and connecting lines with directional arrows link the steps, so anyone can follow the process logically from beginning to end. Teams reach for flowcharts for process documentation, for auditing a process for inefficiencies, and for mapping computer algorithms.

How do I make a flowchart from scratch?
Five steps, whatever tool you use. Define the process you are mapping and give it a title. Decide where the process begins and ends. List the main activities, decisions, and roadblocks in between. Arrange the steps in sequence and draw arrows to show the flow. Then review the finished chart with the people who actually run the process. If the chart outgrows one page, split it and link the parts with a numbered connector circle. In Beautiful.ai, the flowchart template handles the design side while you work through those steps.

What do the flowchart symbols mean?
The conventions are simple. Elongated circles signal the start or end of a process, rectangles represent actions or tasks, and diamonds mark the points where a decision is needed. Keep your shapes, lines, and text consistent, make the start and end points slightly bigger, and hold the palette to three or fewer colors so the chart stays easy to read.

What types of flowcharts can I make?
The big four: workflow diagrams that standardize a system for consistent output, decision flowcharts that visualize your options in a business decision, process maps that troubleshoot and improve efficiency, and cross-functional flowcharts, also called swimlane flowcharts, that document a process across several groups. For flowchart examples and neighboring formats, the Diagrams and Timeline library includes the Process Diagram, Connection Diagrams, and Hub and Spoke Smart Slides.

Is the flowchart maker free?
Beautiful.ai is free to try: every account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Pro or Team plan. After the trial, paid plans are available for individuals, teams, and enterprise organizations. There is no permanently free tier, so you always know exactly what you are getting and when.

Can I use my flowchart in PowerPoint or Google Slides?
Yes. Every deck created in Beautiful.ai can be exported as a PowerPoint file, and slides remain fully editable after export. Google Slides and PDF downloads are available too, so your flowchart arrives as a working slide rather than a pasted picture.

Can my team build flowcharts together?
Yes. Beautiful.ai pairs its AI creation with real-time collaboration, brand controls, and analytics in one platform, so the process owner, the designer, and the presenter can work in the same deck. Themes keep everyone inside the brand while they do it.

When is a dedicated diagramming tool a better fit?
If you are mapping a sprawling, multi-page system architecture or running a formal swimlane audit across departments, a standalone diagramming canvas gives you unlimited space and specialized notation, and it is the right tool for that job. Beautiful.ai's flowchart maker is built for the moment your process needs an audience. The chart lives on a slide, matches the rest of your deck, and presents beautifully without a screenshot in sight.
