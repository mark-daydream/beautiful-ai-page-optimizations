H2: Event planning template FAQ

Is the event planning template editable?

Yes. All 12 slides are Smart Slides. Change your plan, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the event planning template?

Open the template and type your budget, vendors, timeline, guest list, process, and priorities into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the event planning template for me?

Yes. Describe the event planning in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the event planning deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use an event planning presentation?

Use an event planning presentation before a conference, product launch, corporate event, or internal gathering goes live, when goals, logistics, vendors, and responsibilities need to be agreed by everyone involved.
