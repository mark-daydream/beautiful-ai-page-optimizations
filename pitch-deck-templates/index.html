Frequently asked questions

What are presentation templates?

Presentation templates are pre-designed slide decks that give you a strong foundation to start your presentation. Instead of starting with a blank slide, you pick a template designed for your purpose—whether it’s marketing, sales, a pitch, or more—and make it your own with just a few clicks. Each one is thoughtfully crafted to give you a head start while keeping your story front and center.

How are Beautiful.ai’s presentation templates different?

With Beautiful.ai, every template or theme is powered by Smart Slides which means it automatically adapts as you edit and add your content. And now, you can take it even further with AI. Use AI to quickly generate slides from an outline, rewrite text for clarity, or reformat existing content to fit a different style. This means you can start inspired, make quick edits, and let AI help you polish your deck faster than ever.

Can I create my own custom templates or theme?

Users on a Team plan can easily create their own custom themes by designing a deck once and saving it as a template for future use. This is perfect for fast-paced teams who want to maintain consistent branding across multiple presentations. Your saved theme will keep layouts, colors, fonts, and design elements intact, so you can spin up new decks quickly while staying on-brand.

How do presentation templates work?

You start by picking a theme based on your goal, then edit each slide and add your own content. As you customize, the slides automatically adapt and reformat, ensuring everything stays beautifully aligned and professionally designed. This frees you from tedious design work so you can focus on telling a clear, compelling story.

What are Smart Slides?

Smart Slides are the foundation of Beautiful.ai’s presentation platform. They’re interactive, intelligent slide layouts that automatically adapt as you edit, so your content always stays beautifully aligned and professionally designed. No more nudging text boxes or resizing images. Smart Slides keep every slide consistent and clean.

Can I use Beautiful.ai with my company’s brand?

Yes. With Themes and Brand Control, you can define your brand’s fonts, colors, logos, and layouts once—and every presentation will follow those rules automatically. For teams and enterprises, you can also lock design elements, share branded templates, and manage editing permissions to ensure every deck stays consistent and professional.

What should a pitch deck template include?

A strong pitch deck template includes slides for your market opportunity, product solution, traction, business model, team, and funding ask. Every template in this category gives you those building blocks, and Smart Slides adapt the design automatically as you add your content.

Which investor pitch deck template should I use for fundraising?

Match the template to your stage. The startup pitch deck template works for a first investor conversation, the seed round template is built for initial funding, and the Series B template helps you make the case for growth capital. To size your market for investors, add the Total Addressable Market (TAM) template.

Can I see examples of famous startup pitch decks?

Yes. This category includes recreated decks from companies like Airbnb, Uber, Netflix, Dropbox, Snapchat, and LinkedIn. Study these pitch deck examples to see how well-known startups structured their stories, then apply the same flow to your own deck.

Are Beautiful.ai’s pitch deck templates free?

Every pitch deck template is available to customize when you start a Beautiful.ai trial, free for 14 days. Choose a template, add your content, and Smart Slides keep the design polished as you edit. When you’re done, you can export your finished deck anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

What are presentation templates?

Presentation templates are pre-designed slide decks that give you a strong foundation to start your presentation. Instead of starting with a blank slide, you pick a template designed for your purpose—whether it’s marketing, sales, a pitch, or more—and make it your own with just a few clicks. Each one is thoughtfully crafted to give you a head start while keeping your story front and center.

How are Beautiful.ai’s presentation templates different?

With Beautiful.ai, every template or theme is powered by Smart Slides which means it automatically adapts as you edit and add your content. And now, you can take it even further with AI. Use AI to quickly generate slides from an outline, rewrite text for clarity, or reformat existing content to fit a different style.
