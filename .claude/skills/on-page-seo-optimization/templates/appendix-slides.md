# Appendix Slide Spec

## Gate pass (7 slides)
1. Cover — Research & Analysis, keyword, URL, date, priority pill
2. Top 10 Organic — competitor cards + client card + opportunity card
3. SERP Features — AI Overview, snippet, PAA, PASF
4. Patterns & Gaps — two-column bullets
5. Keyword Benchmark — data-table
6. Schema Audit — Keep/Revise/Add cards
7. Citability & Takeaway — dark close slide + score table

## Gate fail (3 slides)
1. Cover — SERP Validation Stopped
2. SERP breakdown table
3. Next steps
