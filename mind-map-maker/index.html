Frequently asked questions

What is a mind map?
A mind map is a diagram that organizes ideas around one central concept. The main idea sits in the middle, major themes branch out from it, and smaller details branch again from each theme, so the structure radiates outward like spokes on a wheel. Because that shape mirrors the way people connect ideas, mind maps show up everywhere from brainstorming sessions to lecture notes to project kickoffs.

What is the format of a mind map?
The format is radial. One central node holds the main idea, first-level branches hold the major themes, and second-level branches hold the supporting details. Keep each node to a few words, group related branches with color, and let the map grow outward rather than down the page. In Beautiful.ai, the Hub and Spoke template gives you that structure as a starting point.

Are a mind map, a mind chart, and a mental map different things?
The names are interchangeable. Mind chart and mental map both describe the same radial diagram: a central idea with branches spreading outward. Whichever term your team uses, the structure works the same way, and a mind chart maker and a mind map maker are the same tool.

How do I make a mind map with Beautiful.ai?
Open the Hub and Spoke Smart Slide, name the hub for your central concept, and add a spoke for each major branch. The slide keeps the layout aligned as you add ideas, your theme keeps the colors consistent, and the finished map is already a slide in your deck. From there you can present it, share it, or build the rest of the presentation around it.

Is the mind map maker free?
Beautiful.ai is free to try: every account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Pro or Team plan. After the trial, paid plans are available for individuals, teams, and enterprise organizations. There is no permanently free tier, so you always know exactly what you are getting and when.

Can Beautiful.ai generate a mind map from a text prompt?
No, the AI assistant does not directly generate a new mind map. Start from the Hub and Spoke Smart Slide instead. Smart Slides stay AI-editable while you build, so you can generate, rewrite, and refine the text on your nodes without leaving the slide.

Can my team build a mind map together?
Yes. Beautiful.ai pairs its AI creation with real-time collaboration, brand controls, and analytics in one platform, so the person who mapped the idea and the person who presents it can work in the same deck. Themes keep every branch inside the brand while they do it.

Can I use my mind map in PowerPoint or Google Slides?
Yes. Every deck created in Beautiful.ai can be exported as a PowerPoint file, and slides remain fully editable after export. Google Slides and PDF downloads are available too, so your mind map arrives as a working slide rather than a pasted picture.

When is a dedicated mind mapping tool a better fit?
If you are capturing hundreds of nodes on an infinite canvas, collapsing deep branch hierarchies, or running long research sessions, a dedicated mind mapping tool gives you the space and the specialized controls, and it is the right tool for that job. Beautiful.ai's mind map maker is built for the moment your thinking needs an audience. The map lives on a slide, matches the rest of your deck, and presents beautifully. For neighboring diagrams, the same library includes the Process Diagram and Connection Diagrams, and the flowchart maker.

Why: The spec's four required questions are all here: the how-to recap, the honest trial answer to “is it free,” the export/editability answer, and the concession that routes infinite-canvas jobs away before reframing to presenting. The education layer (what-is, format, naming) is demanded by the SERP: Canva and Miro both run what-is sections, and the format and synonym questions carry the “format of mind map,” “mind chart maker,” and “mental map” supporting intents. The AI question gets the same honest answer the live flowchart page publishes, because no mind map Smart Slide exists to generate. Sources: C7–C17
