AI pitch deck generator FAQs

How does Beautiful.ai's AI pitch deck generator work?
Start with a prompt that describes your company and what you need. Beautiful.ai turns it into a working deck in seconds, then Smart Slides keep every layout aligned as you edit, so refining the draft never breaks the design.

Is there a free AI pitch deck generator trial?
Beautiful.ai offers a 14-day free trial of the Pro or Team plan, with unrestricted access to everything in the plan during the trial. A credit card is required to start, and you can cancel before the trial ends.

What slides should a pitch deck include?
Most investor decks cover the problem, your solution, market size, business model, traction, team, and financials. Beautiful.ai's pitch deck templates give you a proven structure to start from, so you never face a blank deck.

Can I export my pitch deck to PowerPoint?
Yes. You can download your deck as a PDF or an editable PowerPoint file, so your pitch travels wherever investors expect it.

Can I customize an AI-generated pitch deck?
Every slide stays fully editable. Smart Slides adapt the layout as you make changes, and brand controls keep your logo, fonts, and colors consistent while you customize.

Do I have to start from scratch?
No. Choose from professionally designed pitch deck templates, including decks inspired by famous startups like Uber and Airbnb, or generate a custom draft with AI and make it yours.
