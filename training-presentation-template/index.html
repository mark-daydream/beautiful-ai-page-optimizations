H2: Training template FAQ

Is the training template editable?

Yes. All 13 slides are Smart Slides. Change your material, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the training template?

Open the template and type your objectives, components, phases, schedule, and tools into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the training template for me?

Yes. Describe the training in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the training deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use a training presentation?

Use a training presentation for a new-hire session, a coaching session, or a webinar, whenever a speech needs structure, a visual aid, and a message that sticks after the session ends.

Why: Answers are product facts in this page’s own nouns; the “when should I use” answer derives from the kept overview copy, so it stays unique to the page. No answer claims the template or the product is free. Mirrored in the FAQPage schema below.
