H2: Architecture project template FAQ

Is the architecture project template editable?

Yes. All 11 slides are Smart Slides. Change your project, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the architecture project template?

Open the template and type your site analysis, design philosophy, floor plans, timeline, and budget into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the architecture project template for me?

Yes. Describe the architecture project in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the architecture project deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use an architecture project presentation?

Use an architecture project presentation from the first design proposal to the final review, whenever clients, review boards, or internal teams need the concept, the plans, and the rationale in one visual story.
