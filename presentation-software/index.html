How does Beautiful.ai use artificial intelligence (AI)?

Beautiful.ai uses advanced AI and machine learning models to help you create, structure, and enhance presentations faster. AI features can generate slide content, rewrite or summarize text, and apply design logic automatically — but you always stay in control.

AI outputs are suggestions, not final answers. Because machine learning is probabilistic, results may occasionally include errors or need review. Users should verify content accuracy before presenting. See the AI Policy for more details.

Can I try Beautiful.ai before committing to a paid plan?

Yes — you can begin with a 14-day free trial of the Pro or Team plan, which gives full access to those features including unlimited slides, AI-powered content generation, and analytics. After the trial, billing begins unless you cancel before the end of the trial period.

Can teams collaborate in real time?

Absolutely. Beautiful.ai was built for modern collaboration. Multiple teammates can edit the same deck simultaneously, comment on slides, and share updates instantly. Shared workspaces, asset libraries, and real-time syncing keep everyone aligned—no more version chaos or off-brand slides.

How does Beautiful.ai help me save time?

Every feature is designed to remove friction from deck creation:

  • Smart Slides automate formatting and design
  • Reusable templates eliminate starting from scratch
  • Brand themes ensure you never fix colors or fonts again

Does Beautiful.ai support multimedia?

Yes. You can embed videos, images, GIFs, and charts directly into slides, or browse our built-in stock image and video libraries to find the perfect visuals without leaving the platform.

What's included in the Team plan vs. the Enterprise plan?

Team is for smaller organizations that need collaboration, shared themes, and brand control. Enterprise is for organizations of 20+ that require advanced security, audit logs, SSO, configurable guardrails, and dedicated onboarding. Both include everything in Pro, plus shared workspaces and admin controls for brand consistency at scale.

Can AI replace designers?

No—and that's not the goal. AI in Beautiful.ai helps professionals design faster and smarter, not replace creative expertise. It removes repetitive formatting work and provides a structured starting point—freeing you up to focus on story, structure, and strategy.

What happens to my content and data?

You own the content you create. Beautiful.ai securely processes your presentation data only to deliver our services—never to train third-party AI models. Enterprise customers benefit from additional safeguards like Single Sign-On (SSO), SCIM provisioning, and audit logs. For full details, see our Privacy Policy and Security Overview.

Frequently asked questions

How is Beautiful.ai different from traditional presentation tools like PowerPoint or Google Slides? KW

Beautiful.ai reimagines presentation software for modern professionals. Instead of starting with a blank slide, you start with Smart Slides—intelligent layouts that automatically align, resize, and animate as you edit.

With Beautiful.ai, you can:

  • Generate decks with AI from just a prompt or outline
  • Instantly apply your brand with locked colors, fonts, and logos
  • Collaborate live with teammates in shared workspaces
  • Share securely with view-only links and engagement analytics

The result? Decks that are faster to build, easier to maintain, and always beautifully on-brand—even without design help.

How does Beautiful.ai use artificial intelligence (AI)?

Beautiful.ai uses advanced AI and machine learning models to help you create, structure, and enhance presentations faster. AI features can generate slide content, rewrite or summarize text, and apply design logic automatically — but you always stay in control.

AI outputs are suggestions, not final answers. Because machine learning is probabilistic, results may occasionally include errors or need review. Users should verify content accuracy before presenting. See the AI Policy for more details.

Can I try Beautiful.ai before committing to a paid plan?

Yes — you can begin with a 14-day free trial of the Pro or Team plan, which gives full access to those features including unlimited slides, AI-powered content generation, and analytics. After the trial, billing begins unless you cancel before the end of the trial period.

Can teams collaborate in real time?

Absolutely. Beautiful.ai was built for modern collaboration. Multiple teammates can edit the same deck simultaneously, comment on slides, and share updates instantly. Shared workspaces, asset libraries, and real-time syncing keep everyone aligned—no more version chaos or off-brand slides.

How do I share or present my deck?

Because Beautiful.ai is online presentation software, your decks live in the cloud. Present live directly from Beautiful.ai, share via secure link for asynchronous viewing, or embed decks on your website. With built-in Viewer Analytics, you can track who's viewed your deck, what slides they engaged with, and how long they spent, so you know what resonates. KW

How does Beautiful.ai help me save time?

Every feature is designed to remove friction from deck creation:

  • AI generation speeds up first drafts
  • Smart Slides automate formatting and design
  • Reusable templates eliminate starting from scratch
  • Brand themes ensure you never fix colors or fonts again

Most users report cutting deck creation time by over 70%.

Does Beautiful.ai support multimedia?

Yes. You can embed videos, images, GIFs, and charts directly into slides, or browse our built-in stock image and video libraries to find the perfect visuals without leaving the platform.

What's included in the Team plan vs. the Enterprise plan?

Team is for smaller organizations that need collaboration, shared themes, and brand control. Enterprise is for organizations of 20+ that require advanced security, audit logs, SSO, configurable guardrails, and dedicated onboarding. Both include everything in Pro, plus shared workspaces and admin controls for brand consistency at scale.

Can AI replace designers?

No—and that's not the goal. AI in Beautiful.ai helps professionals design faster and smarter, not replace creative expertise. It removes repetitive formatting work and provides a structured starting point—freeing you up to focus on story, structure, and strategy.

What happens to my content and data?

You own the content you create. Beautiful.ai securely processes your presentation data only to deliver our services—never to train third-party AI models. We comply with global privacy and security regulations, including SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, CCPA, and PCI, and implement industry-standard encryption and access controls. Enterprise customers benefit from additional safeguards like Single Sign-On (SSO), SCIM provisioning, and audit logs. For full details, see our Privacy Policy and Security Overview.

How do I get started?

Sign up for a 14-day free trial and start building. Switching to a new presentation tool is easiest when you start from something real, so create from a template, generate with AI, or import an outline. Teams and enterprises can request a demo for customized onboarding and brand setup. KW

How does subscription billing and cancellation work?

Annual subscriptions are billed once per year and automatically renew unless cancelled, while monthly subscriptions (where available) are non-refundable and billed each month. You may cancel your subscription via the billing section of your account — will continue until the end of your current billing cycle. Refunds are not provided for monthly subscriptions; annual subscriptions may be eligible for partial refunds within 24 hours of purchase or renewal.

What are Smart Slides?

Smart Slides are the foundation of Beautiful.ai, and what sets it apart from other slide presentation software. They're responsive, intelligent layouts that automatically adapt your content as you edit, applying professional design rules in real time. You focus on storytelling and structure, while Smart Slides handle alignment, spacing, and polish behind the scenes. KW

Beautiful.ai offers three main tiers: Pro for individuals at $12/month (billed annually), Team for 2–20 users at $40 per user/month (billed annually) (or $50/month if billed monthly), and custom-priced Enterprise plans for organizations of 20+ users requiring advanced security and controls.
