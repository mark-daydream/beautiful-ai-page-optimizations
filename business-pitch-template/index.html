H2: Business pitch template FAQ

Is the business pitch template editable?

Yes. All 11 slides are Smart Slides. Change your pitch, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the business pitch template?

Open the template and type your problem, solution, market, business model, traction, team, and ask into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the business pitch template for me?

Yes. Describe the business pitch in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the business pitch deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use a business pitch presentation?

Use a business pitch presentation when you ask investors, partners, or stakeholders to back an idea, and you need the problem, the solution, the market, and the ask in a story that ends with a clear request.
