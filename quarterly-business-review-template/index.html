H2: Pro tips to build your own QBR

As you use this template to build your own quarterly business review presentation, keep these tips in mind:

Organize content logically Start with an overview of key highlights and objectives, don’t dive into the details right away.

Include strategic callouts Use callout boxes, annotations, or text overlays to highlight additional insights. Draw attention to important information in ways that guides the viewer’s focus toward critical aspects.

Set time for feedback or questions You likely have a lot of information to cover in a short amount of time. Have viewers save questions for the end, where you have slides dedicated to discussion.

Provide metrics in visually compelling ways With Beautiful.ai’s interactive graphs and charts, you not only enhance the credibility of your review but make it easier for stakeholders to grasp complex information quickly.

H2: Quarterly business review template FAQ

Is the quarterly business review template editable?

Yes. All 17 slides are Smart Slides. Change the numbers, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own data to the quarterly business review template?

Open the template and type your goals, KPIs, and results into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your numbers on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the quarterly business review template for me?

Yes. Describe the quarter in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the QBR deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use a quarterly business review?

Hold one at the end of each quarter to compare performance against the goals you set, gather feedback from the team, and agree on priorities for the next quarter, whether the audience is a customer, your executive team, or internal partners.
