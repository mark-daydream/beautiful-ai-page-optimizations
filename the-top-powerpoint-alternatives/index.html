2. Prezi

Prezi's presentation software is a true alternative to PowerPoint. A presentation platform centered around a zoomable, non-linear canvas, enabling more interactive and engaging storytelling. Rather than design standard slide decks like in PowerPoint, Prezi users create animated, non-sequential presentations that highlight topics and uncover subtopics with an engaging zoom feature. The PowerPoint-alternative software is an enticing option for creating visual presentations, but it's probably better suited for experienced presentation designers thanks to its unique design.

3. Powtoon

Powtoon— a British company that got their name by combining "PowerPoint" and "cartoon"— launched in 2012 as a more animated approach to otherwise stale presentations. It's a platform for creating presentations and explainer videos with drag-and-drop tools and a library of characters, templates, and animations. The app focuses on animation and storytelling, offering a solution for creating engaging video presentations rather than static slides.

4. Gamma

Gamma is a modern document, webpage, and presentation tool, blending the format of slide decks with interactive elements, such as embeds and carousels. A hybrid approach that bridges the gap between different content from web design to presentations. Their AI allows users to add prompts and modify elements of design and copy that need quick edits.

5. Visme

Visme's PowerPoint-alternative presentation software is a design tool for presentations, infographics, and other visual content creation, with templates and branding features for professionals. The easy-to-use cloud-based tool allows users to move elements around the workspace, or add new content by dragging and dropping it into place. Visme users can build presentations from scratch by starting with a blank canvas, or they can customize a variety of templates. Their AI technology allows you to create or edit content with a prompt.

6. Zoho Show

Zoho Show is a cloud-based presentation tool that offers collaborative features and seamless integration with the Zoho ecosystem. Users can create, edit, share and deliver presentations from anywhere. Zoho Show puts a strong emphasis on real-time collaboration and integration with Zoho's suite of business tools, making it ideal for teams using Zoho products. The program recognizes the task the user is conducting, and automatically provides the most relevant options to reduce overall clutter on the screen. The presentation software also features templates for a variety of topics and purposes.

7. Haiku Deck

Different from PowerPoint, Haiku Deck prioritizes simplicity and aesthetics, catering to users who want quick, polished results without extensive customization. It's a web-based app that uses artificial intelligence to transform ideas into eye-catching slide decks. This PowerPoint-alternative presentation tool is designed for users who want to craft presentations with simple messages using basic templates. And the drag-and-drop interface makes it a tool even amateur designers can master in no time at all.

8. Pitch

The platform focuses on collaboration and real-time editing for teams, with an emphasis on modern, startup-style aesthetics. Plus, Pitch's templates and customizable presentation styles allow users to easily stay on brand throughout their entire pitch decks. Pitch presentations can be integrated with Google Sheets, Google Analytics and other productivity programs to help seamlessly connect workflows.

9. Google Slides

Do you have a Google account? If so, then you already have access to the search giant's own PowerPoint-alternative presentation software. Anyone with a Google account can access Google Slides from within the company's suite of productivity applications. In terms of its user interface, Google Slides resembles PowerPoint in many ways, but presentations can be shared among Google users, making it a popular collaboration tool. Google Slides has its limits, however, since only a limited library of shapes and icons are available to add without seeking and importing the images from elsewhere. Google Slides may require more design skills than some of the other PowerPoint alternatives.

10. Slidebean

Similar to Beautiful.ai, Slide Bean's user-friendly PowerPoint alternative software uses artificial intelligence to assist in creating a professional-level visual presentation design. The free program similar to PowerPoint features customizable templates, or users can design their slide deck from scratch. While presentations can be edited in outline or design modes, users are limited to the structure of the content blocks, so they can't customize as much as they can with other alternative programs to PowerPoint.
