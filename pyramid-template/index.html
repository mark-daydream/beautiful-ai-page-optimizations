H2: Pro Tips for using a Pyramid Slide Template

When creating a pyramid slide, here are a few things to consider.

Label your pyramid appropriately. Let each section in your pyramid represent one category. Label it appropriately so your audience understands how the pyramid is organized.

Only use a pyramid for topics that are connected. Your topic has to make sense when arranged in a pyramid. How new employees can get promoted makes sense for a pyramid; how every department relates to each other does not.

Use different colors. Make the differences between your pyramid levels even clearer by assigning different colors to each one.

Limit the number of sections. Too many sections in your pyramid will look crowded and complicated. If five sections aren’t enough, consider breaking your info into separate pyramid templates.

H2: Pyramid template FAQ

Is the pyramid template editable?

Yes. The template is a fully editable Smart Slide. Change your levels and their labels, and the layout adapts automatically as you work.

How do I add my own data to the pyramid template?

Open the template and type your levels and their labels in, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places them on the slide.

Can AI fill in the pyramid template for me?

Yes. Describe the pyramid you want to show and the AI assistant fills the template with your levels and their labels in place. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the pyramid template to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Slides export to PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link directly from Beautiful.ai.

When should I use a pyramid?

Use a pyramid when items build on one another from smallest to largest, such as a hierarchy, the steps in a process, or a difference in quantity across categories. If the items are unrelated, a list or a bar graph is the better choice.
