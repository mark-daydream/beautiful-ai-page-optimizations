# Brand Voice Profile — Beautiful.ai

Sampled: homepage, /about, /presentation-maker (2026-06-02)

## Voice & tone
- **Confident, witty, slightly irreverent.** "Remember that time you had to make a presentation and it was really easy…? NEITHER DO WE." Self-aware, a little cheeky.
- **Fighter-brand energy.** They explicitly call themselves "a fighter brand" that values "inclusive debate." Punchy, opinionated.
- **Benefit-led, outcome-first.** Copy leads with what you get: "work-ready slides with fewer edits," "75% less time," "10x better."
- **Human and empathetic to the pain.** Names the midnight-before-the-deck struggle, "banging their heads against the wall."
- **Second person, active voice.** Heavy "you/your." Imperatives: "Move from prompt to deck," "Skip the formatting."

## Sentence mechanics
- Short, declarative, punchy. Frequent one-line value statements.
- Em dashes appear in source copy — but our deliverable's quality gate forbids em dashes, so rewrite with commas/periods.
- Light alliteration and rhythm ("at superspeed," "prompt to polished deck").

## Lexicon / proprietary terms (preserve verbatim)
- **Beautiful.ai** (always this casing)
- **Smart Slides** / **Smart Slide layouts**
- **Frankendeck** (their coined term for a cluttered, over-built deck)
- **presentation maker** / **AI Presentation Maker**
- **Themes** (capitalized feature)
- Founder: **Mitch Grasso**; prior co.: **Sliderocket**

## Values (for framing, not literal copy)
Design Forward · Detail Oriented · Spirited · Collaborative

## Do / Don't for our draft
- DO: keep it confident, benefit-first, second person, professional-but-human.
- DO: preserve branded feature names exactly.
- DON'T: use em dashes, AI-slop filler ("in today's fast-paced world," "unlock," "elevate," "delve"), or rename any branded term.
