H2: Competitive analysis template FAQ

Is the competitive analysis template editable?

Yes. All 14 slides are Smart Slides. Change your competitor research, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the competitive analysis template?

Open the template and type your competitors, SWOT, KPI comparison, market share, and pricing into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the competitive analysis template for me?

Yes. Describe the competitive analysis in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the competitive analysis deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use a competitive analysis presentation?

Use a competitive analysis presentation when you need to show where you stand against the field, from a strategy review to a product launch, and want the strengths, weaknesses, pricing, and share of each competitor side by side.

Why: Answers are product facts in this page’s own nouns; the “when should I use” answer derives from the kept overview copy, so it stays unique to the page. No answer claims the template or the product is free. Mirrored in the FAQPage schema below.
