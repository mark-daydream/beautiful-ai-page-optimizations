Frequently asked questions

What is a timeline?
A timeline is a visual representation marking all major events over a period of time. Everything from project plans to status updates to next steps can be communicated with one, and it is one of the most effective ways to give your audience confidence that a plan has been thoroughly vetted and is well-articulated.

How do I make a timeline for a presentation?
Decide the window of time you are covering, list the events that belong on it, and put them in order. Then pick the format that fits: timelines can be horizontal, vertical, interactive, or chronological, and the right choice depends on what you want your audience to take away. In Beautiful.ai, the timeline template handles layout and spacing while you work through those decisions.

What are timeline slides used for?
The most common jobs are project planning and milestones, status updates, historical context, next steps and future roadmaps, and quarterly or annual reviews. Timelines also arrange nonlinear events into chronological order, which makes them useful well beyond project work.

Is the timeline maker free?
Beautiful.ai is free to try: every account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Pro or Team plan. After the trial, paid plans are available for individuals, teams, and enterprise organizations, so you always know exactly what you are getting and when.

Can I rearrange milestones after I add them?
Yes. Every milestone drags freely along the timeline, and the slide adjusts the arrangement for the best fit as you reposition it. Auto Arrange Milestones distributes everything evenly with one click, and each milestone can switch between Text, Marker, Numbered, Lettered, or Media and Text styles at any point.

Can I use my timeline in PowerPoint or Google Slides?
Yes. Export your deck as a PowerPoint file and every element stays editable, including your presentation notes. Google Slides and PDF downloads are available too, so the timeline arrives as a working slide rather than a pasted picture.

Can AI generate my timeline?
It can. Prompt the AI assistant with the type of timeline you are looking to create and it comes together automatically. Add context by uploading documents or data, refine the copy on the slide with AI, and have it generate speaker notes from your content when you are ready to present.

When is a dedicated timeline tool a better fit?
If you are building a zoomable research timeline with hundreds of dated events, a dedicated timeline canvas gives you the infinite scroll and archival depth that job needs. Beautiful.ai's timeline maker is built for the timeline with an audience. The milestones live on a slide, match the deck around them, and present beautifully without a screenshot in sight.
