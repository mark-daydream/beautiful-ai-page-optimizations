A Gantt chart is a visual view of tasks scheduled over time. Invented by Henry Gantt, an American mechanical engineer, it's a type of bar chart that shows the start and finish dates of several elements of a project that include: what the project tasks are, who is working on each task, how long each task will take, and how tasks group together, overlap, and link with each other.

Gantt charts are usually used for:

  • Project management to track tasks and milestones
  • Marketing planning to ensure deadlines are met
  • Product roadmaps to manage deadlines and deliverables.

The Beautiful.ai Gantt chart template is the perfect tool to show all elements of a complex project in a balanced, easy to read graphic.

Why: The overview copy is real, useful content and stays word for word; only the heading changes and the block moves below the new template layer.

Pro Tips for creating a Gantt Chart

Make your Gantt chart appealing by:

Keeping it simple. Project management is inherently complex, so keep your chart simple by using a color scheme with cool colors like blues or greens.

Using a sans serif font. Again, limit the complexity of your Gantt chart by using basic, easily readable fonts like Arial or Calibri. You can use free Google fonts which come with every Beautiful.ai account.

Adding icons and arrows to your chart. Highlight important tasks using our vast library of icons. This will make your chart easier to read and more visually appealing.

Add context with AI Add additional context to your prompt by uploading documents, or data, and the AI will use that to generate a Gantt chart that fits your story.

Gantt chart template FAQ

Is the Gantt chart template editable?

Yes. The template is a fully editable Smart Slide: add tasks, adjust start and finish dates, and reassign owners, and the bars stay aligned as you work.

How do I add my own tasks?

Open the template and type in each task, its owner, and its start and finish dates, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your schedule on the slide.

Can AI fill it in for me?

Yes. Describe the project and its phases, and the AI assistant fills the template with tasks, owners, and dates in place. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Slides export to PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link directly from Beautiful.ai.

When should I use a Gantt chart?

A Gantt chart works best when a project’s tasks need to be seen over time: tracking tasks and milestones, planning marketing against deadlines, or managing a product roadmap’s deliverables. Bars show each element’s start and finish dates in one balanced graphic.
