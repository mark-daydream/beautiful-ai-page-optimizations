H2: Pro Tips for Bar Graph Template Slides (grammar-gate edit from “Pro Tips for Bar Graph Template Slide”)

When filling out your bar graph template slide, here are a few things to consider:

Use it to track long-term changes. Vertical bar graphs are great for comparing different groups that change over a long period of time. Small or short-term changes may not be as obvious in bar graph form.

Don’t be afraid to play with design. You can use one bar graph template slide to display a lot of information, as long as you differentiate between data sets. Use colors, spacing, and labels to make the differences obvious.

Use a horizontal graph when necessary. If your data labels are long, a horizontal bar graph may be easier to read and organize than a vertical bar graph.

Don’t use a horizontal graph to track time. A vertical bar graph makes more sense when graphing data over time, since the x-axis is usually read from left to right.

H2: Bar graph template FAQ

Is the bar graph template editable?

Yes. Open it with Use this template and every element is editable in the Beautiful.ai editor: the categories, the values, the axis labels, and the colors and fonts around them. Smart Slides keep the layout aligned as you make changes.

How do I add my own data?

Click Use this template, then enter your categories and values directly in the chart. You can also upload a data file or describe the data and let AI fill the graph.

Can AI fill it in for me?

Yes. The AI assistant can rewrite the text on the slide, summarize a complex data set into digestible chunks, and call out trends or relationships it finds in the data.

Can I export to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. Share a live link, present directly from Beautiful.ai, or export the finished slide to PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF.

When should I use a bar graph?

Use a bar graph to compare data over time, show the relationship between two groups, or point out a pattern in the numbers. Long labels read better on the horizontal version.
