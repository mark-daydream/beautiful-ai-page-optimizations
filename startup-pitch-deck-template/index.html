H2: Tips for building your Startup Pitch Deck

Consider the tips below when creating your startup pitch deck.

Create different pitch decks One pitch deck for in-person presentations will rely mainly on visuals, while an email version can help give your presentation more context with text.

Be brief and concise A pitch deck should be clear, compelling, and straightforward. About 10 to 20 slides should be enough to hit all the key points.

Make it visual and on-brand Investors want to get a feel for the company and the energy behind your team and your offers. Don’t be afraid to show personality with graphics, visuals, and brand colors/fonts to show just how much your startup stands out.

Leave investors wanting more Your startup pitch deck should spark interest, leaving investors hungry for more information about your company. Don’t give away too much in your presentation.

H2: Startup pitch deck template FAQ

Is the startup pitch deck template editable?

Yes. All 14 slides are Smart Slides. Change your story, rename the sections, reorder or remove slides, and the layout adapts as you work.

How do I add my own content to the startup pitch deck template?

Open the template and type your problem, product, market, team, and financial projections into the slides, or upload a document or data file and Beautiful.ai places your content on the slides. Charts can also link to a spreadsheet for fast edits and live updates.

Can AI fill in the startup pitch deck template for me?

Yes. Describe the startup pitch deck in a prompt, attach a document or data file for context, and Create with AI drafts the outline and then the slides. You can keep editing everything afterward.

Can I export the startup pitch deck deck to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

Yes. On Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans the deck exports to editable PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF, and you can also present or share a live link straight from Beautiful.ai. Transitions and animations play in Beautiful.ai and do not carry over on export.

When should I use a startup pitch deck?

Use a startup pitch deck when you are raising a seed round or a Series A, B, or C, or any time you present the company to investors. Keep one version for each audience and leave them wanting more.
