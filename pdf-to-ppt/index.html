Convert a PDF to PPT in three steps

Skip the conversion queue. Create with AI turns your PDF into a designed, editable presentation in one guided flow.

1. Upload your PDF
In Beautiful.ai, click Create Presentation and choose Create with AI. Click the plus icon to add your PDF as source material. Documents and links work too.

2. Shape the outline
Beautiful.ai reads your content and generates a slide-by-slide outline before designing anything. Edit titles and copy, reorder or remove slides, and add sections until the story is right.

3. Export as PowerPoint
Generate your deck, then export it as an editable PowerPoint file. Every element stays editable in PowerPoint, speaker notes included.

New Feature section · H2 + 3 subsections
New

Why convert your PDF with AI instead of a file converter

Slides, not snapshots
A traditional converter traces each PDF page into a static slide. You get the right file type and the same frozen layout. Beautiful.ai rebuilds your content on Smart Slides, intelligent layouts that adapt automatically as you edit, so the deck behaves like it was designed from scratch.

Every slide stays editable
The point of converting PDF to PowerPoint is to keep working on it. Exports from Beautiful.ai keep every element editable in PowerPoint, down to your speaker notes. Prefer another format? Export to Google Slides or PDF as well.

The outline keeps you in control
Conversion should not mean surprises. Because Create with AI drafts a slide-by-slide outline first, you decide what makes the cut before a single slide is designed. Trim the appendix, merge sections, or expand a key point with pasted content.

Why: The family's feature block is an H2 + 3 H3 cards; these three carry the whole positioning — rebuild vs trace, editability, control. “Traditional converter” copy stays generic (no named competitor, no imported competitor facts). Internal links follow the family's pattern (/smart-slides, /presentation-maker). Sources: C2, C3, C4, C6, C7, C12

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a PDF to PPT with Beautiful.ai?
Start a new presentation with Create with AI and attach your PDF using the plus icon. Review the outline the AI drafts from your content, generate the deck, and export an editable PowerPoint file when it is ready.

Is the PDF to PPT converter free?
Beautiful.ai is free to try: every account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Pro or Team plan, and editable PowerPoint export is included on those plans. After the trial, a paid subscription keeps your access going.

Can I edit the converted PowerPoint file?
Yes, that is the point. Every deck created in Beautiful.ai can be exported as a PowerPoint file where all elements remain editable in PowerPoint, including your presentation notes. No flattened images, no locked text boxes.

Will my PDF's formatting be preserved?
No, and that is deliberate. Beautiful.ai does not trace your PDF pixel for pixel; it rebuilds the content into designed slides, and the outline step lets you shape what carries over. If you need an exact visual copy of the PDF, a traditional file converter is the better tool. If you need a presentation you can actually edit, restyle, and present, this is.

Can I convert scanned PDFs?
[NEEDS CUSTOMER INPUT] Does Create with AI extract text from scanned or image-only PDFs (OCR)? No support documentation found; 3 of 8 competitors answer this on-page. Answer honestly or drop the question.

What formats can I export to besides PowerPoint?
Beautiful.ai exports to editable PowerPoint, PowerPoint images, Google Slides, and PDF, including a compressed PDF option. One note on Google Slides: embedded videos, audio, animations, and transitions are not supported there and will be lost on export.

What happens to my PDF after I upload it?
[NEEDS CUSTOMER INPUT] File security and retention policy for uploads (encryption, storage, deletion). 4 of 8 competitors answer this on-page; no customer source found. Security/legal should supply the accurate answer.

The formatting answer concedes honestly — a claim of pixel-perfect conversion would be false and would attract the wrong clicks. The free answer states the trial and the plan gating precisely.
