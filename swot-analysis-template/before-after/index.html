Title tag

Optimized
BeforeSWOT Analysis Template and Best Practices | Beautiful.ai | Beautiful.ai
AfterSWOT Analysis Template for Presentations | Beautiful.ai

Two fixes in one: the doubled “| Beautiful.ai” suffix is a Webflow template bug on all 236 pages (one CMS-level edit), and “and Best Practices” dilutes the head term this page should own. “SWOT analysis template” draws 14,800 searches a month, and the page sits at #27.7 today.

Meta description

Optimized
BeforeUse our free SWOT analysis template slide in your next presentation. Highlight strengths, find weaknesses and help your team make the best decisions today!
AfterStart from a designer-made SWOT analysis template, add your analysis by hand or let AI fill it in, then take the finished slide to PowerPoint or Google Slides.

The live description calls the template free. The product is trial-based, so that claim can’t be kept and comes out everywhere on the page. The new description leads with the template and answers the two things searchers check before clicking: can I put my own analysis in, and can I take it to PowerPoint or Google Slides.
