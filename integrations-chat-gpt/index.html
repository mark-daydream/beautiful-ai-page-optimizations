Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Beautiful.ai GPT work?
The Beautiful.ai GPT is a custom GPT for presentations that lives inside ChatGPT. Describe the presentation you need, answer its clarifying questions, and it builds a finished deck on Beautiful.ai's Smart Slide layouts. Open the result in Beautiful.ai to keep editing, apply your Theme, and share.

Can ChatGPT make presentations?
Yes. With the Beautiful.ai GPT, ChatGPT can turn a prompt or an outline into a full Beautiful.ai presentation instead of a wall of text. The deck is built on Smart Slide layouts, so it arrives designed, not just drafted.

How do I add the Beautiful.ai GPT?
In ChatGPT, open Explore GPTs and search for Beautiful.ai. Start a chat with the GPT, describe your presentation, and follow its questions. The GPT lives in ChatGPT, and your decks live in Beautiful.ai.

What can I create with the Beautiful.ai GPT?
Any deck you'd normally build in Beautiful.ai: pitch decks, board updates, QBRs, recap decks, product launches. From pitching to strategizing, the GPT gets you structured outlines and polished content instantly.

Can ChatGPT edit my existing Beautiful.ai decks?
The GPT is built for drafting new presentations. For hands-on changes to any deck, open it in Beautiful.ai; drafting happens in ChatGPT, and polish happens in the editor built for it.

Is my content secure?
Your presentations live in your Beautiful.ai account, and Beautiful.ai's security program is SOC 2 Type II certified and aligned with GDPR, CCPA, and PCI.

Do I need a paid Beautiful.ai account?
You need a Beautiful.ai account to open and edit your generated deck. If you're new, Pro and Team each come with a 14-day free trial, so you can run the whole prompt-to-deck workflow before you pay.

How is this different from the Beautiful.ai Claude Connector?
Beautiful.ai also connects to Claude, alongside Slack, PowerPoint, and Webex. In Claude, Beautiful.ai runs as a Verified Connector built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP); in ChatGPT, it works as the custom Beautiful.ai GPT. Same idea in both places: prompt in chat, get designed slides, finish in Beautiful.ai.

Why: The FAQ is the family's SEO depth layer (the PowerPoint sibling runs 8 link-dense questions) and this page has none. “Can ChatGPT make presentations?” answers a supporting keyword in the searcher's own words, and the first question places “custom gpt for presentations” in its natural home. Every answer is grounded in the page's own live copy or site-wide facts; connector-only capabilities (deck search, Themes, export via chat, OAuth) are deliberately NOT claimed for the GPT. The Claude-comparison question is the cross-link twin of the Claude draft's ChatGPT question, and it is the only place MCP appears — describing the Claude side.
