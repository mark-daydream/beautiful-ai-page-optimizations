Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Beautiful.ai ChatGPT app work?
MCP, or Model Context Protocol, is a standard that lets AI assistants connect with external applications using approved tools. The Beautiful.ai app uses it to give ChatGPT scoped access to your Beautiful.ai account, so it can search decks, create presentations from structured outlines, generate from templates, and export finished files, all on your behalf.

Can ChatGPT make presentations?
Yes. With the Beautiful.ai app connected, ChatGPT can turn a prompt, outline, or pasted document into a full Beautiful.ai presentation, with each slide typed and structured rather than dumped as a wall of text. The deck is built on Smart Slide layouts, so it arrives designed, not just drafted.

What can I create with the app?
Any deck you'd normally build in Beautiful.ai: pitch decks, board updates, QBRs, recap decks, product launches. Start from a blank prompt, from one of your templates, or from a specific look you describe. Generation options cover image sources (AI, web, stock, or none) plus a language setting for decks in other languages.

What happened to the Beautiful.ai GPT?
The Beautiful.ai GPT was the first way to create presentations in ChatGPT, and the Beautiful.ai app is the current ChatGPT presentation app. The app runs on MCP, so it goes further than the GPT did: it can search your existing decks, start from your templates, use your brand Themes, and export finished files, with access you grant through a secure sign-in.

Can ChatGPT edit my existing Beautiful.ai decks?
The app can locate, open, summarize, duplicate from templates, and export decks you already have, but it doesn't edit them slide by slide. For hands-on changes, open the deck in Beautiful.ai; drafting happens in ChatGPT, and polish happens in the editor built for it.

Is the app secure?
Authentication runs on OAuth 2.0, and neither ChatGPT nor the app receives or stores your Beautiful.ai password. The app follows your account's existing permission model, so private decks stay private. Beautiful.ai's security program is SOC 2 Type II certified and aligned with GDPR, CCPA, and PCI.

Do I need a paid Beautiful.ai account?
You need a Beautiful.ai account that can complete the OAuth sign-in. If you're new, Pro and Team each come with a 14-day free trial, so you can run the whole prompt-to-deck workflow before you pay.

How is this different from the Beautiful.ai Claude Connector?
Beautiful.ai also connects to Claude, alongside Slack, PowerPoint, and Webex. Both are built on MCP: the Claude Connector lives in Anthropic's Connectors Directory, while the Beautiful.ai app lives in ChatGPT. Same idea in both places: prompt in chat, get designed slides, finish in Beautiful.ai.

Why: The FAQ is the family's SEO depth layer (the PowerPoint sibling runs 8 link-dense questions) and this page has none. “Can ChatGPT make presentations?” answers a supporting keyword in the searcher's own words. The succession question keeps the Beautiful.ai GPT name alive for users who knew the old product — Mark's checkpoint call — and is worded as succession, not shutdown, since no customer source states the GPT was retired. The Claude-comparison question is the cross-link twin of the Claude draft's ChatGPT question, so each connector page routes searchers to the other.
